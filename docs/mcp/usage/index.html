Usage guide

Practical workflows: studying, revising and tracking progress with your AI app.

You do not need to learn any tool names. Ask your AI app in plain language and it will pick the right Medonex tools. Arabic and English both work.

How your AI app finds content#

Behind the scenes, the usual order is:

  1. list_modules to see your modules and get their ids.
  2. get_module to list the subjects and lectures of one module.
  3. get_lecture to read the parts it needs: notes, flashcards, questions, files or videos.

Asking for specific parts keeps answers fast and focused. For example, “give me only the flashcards for this lecture”.

Example prompts#

Study a lecture#

text
Summarise the notes of my latest lecture in simple points.
Turn the flashcards of this lecture into a short revision list.

Practise#

text
Quiz me with 10 questions from this module, one at a time.
Explain the answer after each question.

Find something#

text
Search my lectures for “cardiac cycle”.
Which lectures did I open most recently?

Track progress#

text
How far am I in this semester?
Show my latest quiz results and tell me what to revise first.

Good habits#

  • Start a new chat per topic so the AI app keeps only the lecture you are working on.
  • Verify medical facts against your official lecture material before an exam.
  • If something is missing, check that you are signed in to the right Medonex account and semester.
