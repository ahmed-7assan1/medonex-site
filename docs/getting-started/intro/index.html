What is Medonex?

An overview of Medonex, the study platform for medical students.

Medonex is a study platform built for medical students. It brings a student’s semester together in one place: modules and lectures, notes, flashcards, question banks, quizzes, a study community and an AI assistant.

This documentation covers the Medonex MCP server in detail and gives an overview of the projects behind the platform. More sections will be added over time.

What can you do with Medonex?#

  • Browse the modules, subjects and lectures of your current semester.
  • Study from lecture notes, flashcards and practice questions.
  • Track your own progress, notes and quiz results.
  • Connect your favourite AI app to your Medonex content through MCP.
