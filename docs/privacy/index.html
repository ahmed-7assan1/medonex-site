Privacy Policy

What MedOneX collects, why, and the choices you have. Last updated November 17, 2026.

MedOneX (medonex.me) is a medical education platform for medical students. It provides lectures, question banks, quizzes, flashcards, a study community, and an AI study assistant (Dr. Q). This policy explains what data MedOneX collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Data we collect#

DataWhenWhy
Name, email address, profile picture, Google account identifierYou sign in with GoogleTo create and display your account
Email address and a hashed passwordYou sign up with emailTo create your account and sign you in
University, faculty and semesterYou complete your profileTo show the content for your semester
Quiz attempts, progress, notes, forum posts and commentsYou use the platformTo save your work and run the community
Messages to the AI assistantYou chat with Dr. QTo generate answers
Technical logs (IP address, device and browser type)AutomaticallyTo keep the service secure and fix errors

We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, contacts, or any other Google service. From Google we receive only the basic profile information used for sign-in.

How we use your data#

  • to create your account, sign you in, and keep your session active
  • to show you the content for your university and semester and to save your progress
  • to provide AI answers, community features, and notifications you ask for
  • to protect the platform against abuse and to diagnose errors
  • to contact you about your account or important changes to the service

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Google user data#

MedOneX uses Google Sign-In only to authenticate you. The name, email, and profile picture received from Google are used to create and display your MedOneX account and are not used for any other purpose, are not shared with advertisers, and are not transferred to anyone except the service providers listed below that are needed to run the platform. MedOneX’s use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Service providers who process data for us#

These providers process data only on our behalf, and we do not give them more data than the feature needs. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

Cookies and storage#

We use cookies and browser storage for your sign-in session, your “remember me” choice, theme preference, and essential app functions. We do not use advertising cookies.

Retention#

We keep your account data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal data from our systems, except where we must keep limited records to meet legal obligations or to prevent abuse. Backups are overwritten on a regular schedule.

Your choices and rights#

You can update your profile in the app, sign out at any time, and revoke MedOneX’s access to your Google account from your Google Account settings (Security → Third-party access). You can ask us to access, correct, export, or delete your data by emailing support@medonex.me from your account email.

Children#

MedOneX is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact#

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions: support@medonex.me.
